How it works

From camera roll to posted in four steps

No timeline scrubbing. No cutting clips by hand. Chop does the tedious part and leaves you the decisions.

01
Upload your videos

Drop one video or fifty. Your footage stays on your device — only a tiny audio track is analysed, so even big files start in seconds.

02
Auto-edit in seconds

Dead air, awkward pauses and every "um" are found and cut automatically. Tune how tight you want it with live sliders — or save your style once in the Cut Lab.

03
Pick your best takes

Said the same line three times? Chop pairs the takes side by side. Preview both, tap the keeper, done. Nothing is ever deleted without you.

04
Export & post

One click renders a clean MP4 with smooth audio joins — right on your device. Download and post straight to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.
